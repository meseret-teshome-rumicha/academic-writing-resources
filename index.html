Understanding Sentences

Build a strong foundation in sentence writing. These resources help you identify sentence components and explore sentence structures in general grammar and scientific and technical English.

What you will practise

  • Identifying complete subjects and complete predicates.
  • Recognising the simple subject and main verb.
  • Combining technical ideas using relative clauses and correct punctuation.
  • Analysing complex and compound-complex sentence structures.
